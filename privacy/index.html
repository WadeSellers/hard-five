75 Hard Crew

Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

The short version. 75 Hard Crew stores your name, the days you tick off, and who is in your crew. That is all. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and nothing is ever sold or shared with anyone. Deleting your account in the app removes all of it.

What the app stores

75 Hard Crew is a shared board for a small group running a daily-task challenge together. To do that, it keeps the following:

WhatWhy
An account identifier from Sign in with AppleSo the app knows which row on the board is yours
Your display nameSo your crew can tell whose row is whose. Taken from Apple the first time you sign in, and you can change it in Settings
Your start date, time zone and row colourYour day number is counted from your start date in your own time zone, so a crew spread across the world still agrees on the board
Which tasks you completed each day, and the time of day you completed themThis is the board. It is the entire point of the app
Your crew's name, its six-character join code, and who is in itSo the right five people see the right board

What the app does not store

  • Your email address. The app asks Apple only for your name. It never requests or receives your email, real or relayed.
  • Any health or fitness data from your phone. The app does not read from Apple Health or from any sensor. A "workout" is a square you tapped, nothing more.
  • Photos. "Progress photo" is a task you tick. The app has no camera access and stores no images.
  • Your location. Your time zone is something you choose in Settings, not something the app senses.
  • Anything for advertising or analytics. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no third-party tracking, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind.

Who can see it

The people in your crew can see your name, your row colour, your day number, and which tasks you have completed each day. That visibility is the product — it is why the app exists.

Nobody outside your crew can see any of it. Crews are not listed, not searchable, and not discoverable. Joining requires a six-character code that somebody in the crew gives you, and the owner can issue a new code at any time, which immediately retires the old one.

Where it is kept

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) on servers in the United States. Google processes it on our behalf as a service provider and does not use it for its own purposes. Google's practices are described in the Firebase Privacy and Security policy.

Everything travels over HTTPS. The app contains no third-party SDKs at all — it talks to Firebase directly over the network and to nothing else.

Deleting your account

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete account. This permanently removes:

  • Every day you have logged with your crew
  • Your row, your name, your start date and your membership
  • Your sign-in record

It also revokes this app's access to your Apple ID, so 75 Hard Crew stops appearing under Settings → Apple Account → Sign in with Apple on your devices.

It cannot be undone and there is no export. If you leave a crew without deleting your account, the days you logged with that crew are removed at that moment too, because once you are off the board nobody — including you — can reach them again.

How long it is kept

Your data stays as long as your account exists. There is no archive, no backup copy retained after deletion, and no analytics store that outlives it.

Children

75 Hard Crew is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The challenge the app is used for involves strenuous daily exercise and is intended for adults.

Not medical advice

75 Hard Crew is a checklist that several people can see. It does not give health, fitness, dietary or medical advice, and it does not check whether any challenge you set up is safe for you. Talk to a doctor before starting a demanding physical programme.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who can see it, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
