What the app is

Up to five people run a daily-task challenge together on one board. A row each: your circle, then six squares. Tap a square when you have done the thing. Your crew watches the row fill in, and you watch theirs. When all six of yours are filled your circle lights up. First one finished that day gets the crown. When everybody is done, the board goes gold.

There is no chat, no feed and no notifications. The board is the whole product.

Common questions

How do I start a crew?

Sign in with Apple, then Start a crew. You will get a six-character join code. Share it with up to four other people.

How do I join one?

Sign in, choose Join a crew, and enter the code somebody gave you.

Someone I do not know joined my crew.

Open Settings → Get a new code if you are the person who started the crew. The old code stops working immediately. People already on the board are not affected.

I ticked a square by mistake.

Press and hold the square to undo it. A single tap only fills; that is deliberate, so a stray tap cannot cost you a day. From the home screen widget, tapping a completed tile opens the app rather than undoing, for the same reason.

Can I change the six tasks?

Yes. Whoever started the crew can edit all six tasks, the length of the challenge, and whether missing a day sends you back to day one. Settings → The challenge. The app ships with a common set as a starting point; a crew can run any daily challenge it likes.

My crewmate is in another country and our days do not line up.

They do, and that is on purpose. Everyone has their own time zone, set in Settings → Your time zone, and each person's row is counted against their own day. The board goes gold when everyone has finished their day, not when everyone has finished the same date.

I am travelling. Will it break my run?

No. Your time zone is something you set, not something the app senses, so flying somewhere does not end your day early. Change it in Settings if you actually move.

Their taps take a moment to show up.

Your own taps are instant. Other people's arrive within about ten seconds while the app is open, and the home screen widget refreshes less often than that. The app has no push notifications.

I missed a day.

If your crew has reset-on-miss turned on, your day number goes back to one. The strip screen shows your whole run, with the missed day marked, so you can see what happened.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. It removes every day you have logged, your row, your profile and your sign-in, and revokes the app's access to your Apple ID. It cannot be undone and there is no export.

Is my data private?

Only your crew can see your board. Crews cannot be searched or listed. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Something is broken

Email wade@wadesellers.com and include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it went wrong. A screenshot helps more than anything else.
